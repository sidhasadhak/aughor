The business

A luxury fashion group selling through four storefronts — Mytheresa, NET-A-PORTER and MR PORTER at full price, YOOX off-price. Sales are each order's GMV, in euros. platforms · orders

What each range is for

  • Week — steer: what left its usual range, and where it sits. Too short to read returns, retention or the quarter's pace as news.
  • Month — review: the month before last's returns have settled; a year earlier, like for like.
  • Year — strategy: storefront and category mix, margin, top clients' share.

What counts as news

A usual week is the eight before it, on the same storefronts. A figure is news when it leaves that range; inside it, one clause at most, however large the change on last week. Weekly sales usually swing about ±7%, the average order ±4%, conversion ±0.1 point. re-measured 28 Oct

Fair comparisons

  • THE OUTNET was sold on 30 April 2026: a year earlier is compared without it, and says so. corporate_events
  • Weeks run Monday to Sunday; a year earlier is 364 days back, so the weekdays match. The fiscal year starts on 1 July. dim_date

The calendar

Singles Day, Black Friday to Cyber Monday, December gifting and the winter and summer sales, from the data's own peak and sale days. dim_date

Read with care

  • A return arrives about 15 days after its order (90% within 21): read returns by the day they arrive; a week's own return rate is early for three weeks. returns × orders
  • A storefront's week is 25–100 orders: name a storefront only when it carries the move; a cell under 30 rows is too few to call.
  • Top clients place about a quarter of Mytheresa's orders and half its sales: a few large orders move its basket. orders
  • Payments never record a failure; prices end Apr 2024, inventory and stylist interactions Jun 2025; marketing spend is per fiscal year for the group. Say so; never read them.

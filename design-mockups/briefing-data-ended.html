Decision for you · where does the data end?

The last row is 17 October 2018, but the data stops being whole in late August

Orders run near 270 a day through mid-August 2018, fall to 187 on 22 August and 99 on 24 August, then 20 stragglers span September and October. Which day an edition anchors to changes what every edition says.

1 · The last row, 17 Oct 2018. Month becomes September 2018, one order worth BRL 145, and the headline reads "GMV fell 100% on August". Day and Week read days holding one order or none.
2 · The last whole day, 22 Aug 2018drawn above · recommended The last day holding at least three quarters of a usual day's orders, where a usual day is the median over the data's final year (201 orders). Month is July (+1.7%). Day and Week sit in the data's last, thinning days, and say so.
3 · The last whole month, July 2018. Every edition reads July or earlier: Day 31 Jul (BRL 45,063, +8.5% on 24 Jul), Week 23–29 Jul (BRL 239,833, +9.3%), Month July. The cleanest figures, but 1–22 August, which looks whole, is never read.

Live data is unchanged in all three: it still reads to today minus its settling lag. The "ended" voice, the paused sends and the withheld Year comparison apply only when the newest whole day is more than a month before today.

The gain is volume: orders rose from 4,153 to 4,652 while the average order held at $85.26 against $84.37. Search traffic carried $34.5K of the $46.2K increase (74.6%) and the Men's department $34.9K (75.5%); across product categories the growth was broad, led by Outerwear & Coats at +$6.4K, with Shorts and Maternity the only declines among the sixteen largest. August is the highest of thirteen months, at the end of a climb from $176.3K in August 2025.

What the data does not show is why Search and Men's moved together in August — campaign spend or stock movements would.

The Overview, redrawn as a control room

A control room answers three questions in order — what's wrong, what's running, what it cost — and every number on it is a door to the list it was computed from. This mockup is live: switch the range, click a tile, drag across the chart, expand a row, accept a proposal.

  • 98% of the "activity" on today's Overview is the automation engine's tick — 1,291 of 1,316 jobs in 24 h. Runs/min: 1.10 with it, 0.10 without.
  • 0 of 7,365 session events carry an agent_id, so per-agent activity cannot be drawn until the emitters are repaired — the plan's first wave.
  • Labels sit at 4.23:1 contrast and captions at 2.76:1 (AA needs 4.5). The tokens that pass exist; the panels just don't use them.

What changed, and why

A
A status rail is the answer to "what's running".One pill per agent with its live state; click scrolls to the row. Background runners sit at the end, dashed, so the tick is visible without ever being counted as an agent.
B
Attention comes first, with an age and one action.The three oldest items from the Attention inbox, inline, with a live timer. Every field product we looked at leads with what needs a human; ours led with tokens/hour.
C
Tiles follow the StatTile spec the repo already has: value → delta vs prior window → sparkline → coverage.Runs counts agents only and says how many background runs it excluded. p50 is computed by the endpoint today and never rendered — it is now. Cost carries its unpriced share or does not appear.
D
One shared axis; every bin is a query.Stacked bars per agent, legend chips that toggle series and double as the swatch key for the table, hover for per-series values, click a bin to filter, drag to brush — the range, the tiles and the table all follow. Stacked because the total is the point.
E
Two lanes, identical columns, no dashes.Charters and custom agents in one grammar (their store gets runs in W0); Automations and Evals in a muted lane below, never summed into the agents' totals. Failures show orphaned restarts beside them, not inside them.
F
Rows expand in place.The last twenty runs as state cells, the model mix, the budget bar and three links — without leaving the table. "Explore ›" opens the drawer with the filtered runs and a link to the Roster page.
G
A drawer, not a modal, and every number is a door.Click any tile: its definition, denominator and coverage, and the URL of the list whose count equals it. That parity is a test in W0, not a promise.
·
Readability is a token rule, not a taste.Values in --t1, labels in --t2, captions in --t3 only at ≥ 12 px, --t4 never for text; state colour uses the ramps' text-grade steps (grn4 / amb4 / red4); the accent means interactive or selected and nothing else.

Before the page can be honest: the data plane (W0)

Every wave in this repo that built a surface before its store existed shipped a panel that rendered empty. Four backend changes make the mockup drawable from real data:

Attribution. Migration 9 on session_events: job_id and charter_id from a job contextvar (so charter identity finally joins model spend), role and fallback lifted out of payload, an index on at. Time. from / to / bucket on the fleet, model-usage and activity endpoints, plus one /obs/timeseries?group=charter|model|kind|role. Drill. /activity filters by model, provider, caller, charter, job_id; a count-parity ratchet ties every tile to the list it opens. Cost. Prices from the provider's own /models list, never hardcoded; unpriced calls stay counted and named. And the automation tick stops folding into "Unassigned kinds" — it becomes a runner the endpoint returns separately.

Decisions — locked 2026-08-17

1 · The automation tick lives in a Background-runners lane on OverviewMuted, one row, never summed into the agents' totals — spend nobody can see is why the row exists.
2 · Dollars appear on OverviewProvider-fed prices at the open RBAC floor, always with the unpriced share stated; /usage keeps billing RBAC for the full ledger.
3 · Attention is a strip on Overview and a layerThe three oldest items inline; the inbox for everything else.
4 · The five layers stay under one shared rangeOverview · Roster · Attention · Activity · Runs — the glossary's names; entity tabs not pursued.
